# Larrikin of SPLORR!!

Yer the Larrikin! Look it up if you want!

Yer job is to find the Macguffin. What's a macguffin? Look that up, too.

On the way, you need to fight things, find weapons and shields, and drink potions.

Good luck.

## Deliberate Design Decisions

- This Metaphor does not protect you from overhealing. You got max health and drink a potion? Have at it! Potion gone... health the same! Hope it was tasty!

## Playing

- **Arrows** turn and step forward, and move through menus.
- **Enter** or **Space** chooses.
- **Escape** opens the game menu (or backs out of a list).
- **G** ground, **I** inventory, **M** map, **F** fight, **R** run.
- On a phone, use the on-screen pad.

**Save Game...** on the game menu saves in yer browser; **Load...** on the main menu picks it back up.

## Now in yer browser

This Metaphor started as a VB.NET terminal game for Windows, made for Dungeon Crawler Jam 2022. In November 2026 it was rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly so it runs in the browser, with the same rules, screens, pictures and beeps. The old Windows download is gone; this browser build replaces it.
