Larrikin of SPLORR!! by TheGrumpyGameDev (VB.NET terminal game, Dungeon Crawler Jam 2022). Rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly in November 2026 by an AI agent (Claude Code) at the author's direction. Arrows turn and step and move through menus; Enter or Space chooses; Escape opens the menu or backs out; G ground, I inventory, M map, F fight, R run. Graphics adapted from the Urizen OneBit tilesets by vurmux (https://vurmux.itch.io/urizen-onebit-tilesets); grim reaper adapted from game-icons.net (Lorc, CC BY 3.0). Font: DINOBYTE by mby (https://mby.itch.io/dinobyte), CC BY 4.0. Feedback by LootLocker.
